# AN-002 Hardware capability (ECR-003)

Shows the hardware can do what the software will ask of it. The software (lsn-node) is a
separate item and is verified by its own tests (TP-001, TP-003, TP-004).

| Question | Need | Hardware can | OK? | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reading rate (REQ-002) | one reading of each quantity at least every 10 s | BME280: one forced measurement of temperature, pressure and humidity at oversampling ×1 takes at most 1.25 + 2.3 + (2.3 + 0.575) + (2.3 + 0.575) = 9.3 ms. SGP40: one raw measurement takes at most 30 ms; its VOC index expects one measurement a second | Yes, by more than 100× | [1] §9.1; [2] Table 8, §3.1 |
| I2C addresses | no two devices on one address | BME280 0x76 or 0x77 (set by SDO; 0x77 on the SparkFun board, as used by lsn-node); SGP40 0x59, fixed | Yes | [1] §6.2; [2] §4.2 |
| Sensor supply | 3.3 V from the Pi | BME280 VDD 1.71 to 3.6 V; SGP40 1.7 to 3.6 V | Yes | [1] Table 1; [2] |
| Power budget | Pi plus sensors within the 5.1 V 2.5 A supply (ECP-0001) | Pi Zero 2 W input power 5 V DC 2.5 A; typical bare-board current 350 mA (unconfirmed: from a summary of [4]); SGP40 at most 4.0 mA; BME280 microamps | Yes | [3]; [4]; [2]; [1] |
| Wi-Fi radio (REQ-004) | a certified radio to send readings | 2.4 GHz IEEE 802.11b/g/n on the board, with modular compliance certification (FCC 2ABCB-RPIZ2, IC 20953-RPIZ2, REQ-014) | Yes | [3]; AN-001 |

## Result
The hardware is capable of REQ-002 and REQ-004 with wide margin. Whether the unit actually does
them is shown by the software item's tests.

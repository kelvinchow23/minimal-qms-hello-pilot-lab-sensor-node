# TP-002 Hardware bring-up, sensor response and unit agreement

Checks the hardware only (ECR-003). The product software (lsn-node) is a separate item with its
own tests. No calibrated instrument is used: the node is for reference values, not absolute ones.

## Setup
- SD card prepared with Raspberry Pi OS and I2C enabled (not part of the build document).
- A way to see the unit's output, e.g. SSH from a laptop.
- `tools/bringup.py` copied to the unit: it prints one reading a second (time, °C, %RH, hPa,
  VOC index). Keep its output as `bringup_<serial>.csv` with the traveller.
- Both units built, lid on, side by side on a bench, away from vents and sun.
- A public weather station near the lab (EQ-003), reporting station or sea-level pressure.

## Steps
- **Step 1 (sensors found):** Run `i2cdetect -y 1`.
  Limit: 0x59 (SGP40) and 0x77 (BME280) both listed. Checks REQ-001.
- **Step 2 (readings):** Start `bringup.py` straight after power-up and note the first readings.
  Limit: 10 to 40 °C, 10 to 90 %RH, 900 to 1100 hPa, and a VOC index is reported. Checks REQ-001.
- **Step 3 (warm-up):** Leave the unit running undisturbed for 1 h. Compare the mean temperature
  of the first minute with the mean of the minute after 1 h. Limit: rise at most 2 °C. Checks
  RISK-001 (sensor not heated by the Pi).
- **Step 4 (temperature):** Cup one hand around the BME280 vents for 60 s.
  Limit: temperature rises by at least 1 °C within 60 s. Checks REQ-003.
- **Step 5 (humidity):** Breathe gently on the BME280 vents twice.
  Limit: humidity rises by at least 5 %RH within 60 s. Checks REQ-003.
- **Step 6 (pressure):** Compare the unit's pressure with the station, converted to the same
  basis (station or sea-level, using the lab's altitude). Limit: within ±5 hPa. Checks REQ-003.
- **Step 7 (VOC):** At least 1 h after power-up, hold an open isopropyl alcohol wipe 10 cm from
  the SGP40 vents for 30 s. Limit: VOC index rises by at least 100 within 60 s. Checks REQ-003.
- **Step 8 (agreement):** Leave both units undisturbed for 30 min and compare their mean
  readings. Limit: within ±1 °C, ±5 %RH and ±2 hPa. Checks REQ-013.
- **Step 9 (agreement, VOC):** Repeat step 7 with the wipe centred between both units.
  Limit: both units' VOC index rises. Checks REQ-013.

## Pass criteria
All steps within limits, on each unit.

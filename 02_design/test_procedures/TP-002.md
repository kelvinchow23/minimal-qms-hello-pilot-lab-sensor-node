# TP-002 Sensor response and unit agreement

No calibrated instrument is used: the node is for reference values, not absolute ones.

## Setup
- Both units running as in TP-001, lid on, side by side on a bench, at least 1 h after power-up
  (the VOC index needs about an hour to settle).
- A public weather station near the lab (e.g. the nearest Environment Canada station),
  reporting station pressure or sea-level pressure.

## Steps
- **Step 1 (temperature):** Cup one hand around the BME280 vents for 60 s.
  Limit: temperature rises by at least 1 °C within 60 s. Checks REQ-003.
- **Step 2 (humidity):** Breathe gently on the BME280 vents twice.
  Limit: humidity rises by at least 5 %RH within 60 s. Checks REQ-003.
- **Step 3 (pressure):** Compare the unit's pressure with the station, converted to the same
  basis (station or sea-level, using the lab's altitude). Limit: within ±5 hPa. Checks REQ-003.
- **Step 4 (VOC):** Hold an open isopropyl alcohol wipe 10 cm from the SGP40 vents for 30 s.
  Limit: VOC index rises by at least 100 within 60 s. Checks REQ-003.
- **Step 5 (agreement):** Leave both units undisturbed for 30 min and compare their mean
  readings. Limit: within ±1 °C, ±5 %RH and ±2 hPa. Checks REQ-013.
- **Step 6 (agreement, VOC):** Repeat step 4 with the wipe centred between both units.
  Limit: both units' VOC index rises. Checks REQ-013.

## Pass criteria
All steps within limits, on each unit.

# AN-001 Operating environment (15 to 35 °C, 20 to 80 %RH non-condensing)

Every component must be rated for the room range plus the temperature rise inside the housing.
Estimated rise: 10 to 20 °C in the Pi bay (about 1 W in a closed box; agent estimate, not
measured; TP-004 logs the CPU temperature). The sensor bay is separated by the partition and
vented, so it runs near room temperature.

Ratings checked against the manufacturers' documents on 2026-11-25 (sources below).

| Part | Rated operating range | Worst case here | OK? | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ECM-0004 Pi Zero 2 W | -20 to +70 °C | up to 55 °C in the Pi bay | Yes | [1] |
| ECM-0001 BME280 | -40 to 85 °C, 0 to 100 %RH | 35 °C, 80 %RH | Yes | Bosch BME280 datasheet |
| ECM-0002 SGP40 | -10 to 50 °C, 0 to 90 %RH non-condensing; humidity compensation up to 30 g/m³ absolute humidity (31 °C dew point) | 35 °C, 80 %RH = about 31.7 g/m³ | Yes, see note | [2] |
| ECM-0003 SHIM, ECW-0001 cable | passive | - | Yes | - |
| ECP-0001 power supply | not stated by the supplier; UL listed, 100 to 240 VAC | 15 to 35 °C room, outside the housing | Yes, see note | [3] |
| ECX-0001 SD card | -25 to 85 °C | up to 55 °C | Yes | SanDisk Extreme spec |
| LSN-0001/0002 printed housing | PLA softens near 55 °C; PETG about 70 °C | up to 55 °C inside | **PLA marginal** | Material data |

## Result
Meets REQ-006, with one condition carried to the G2 review:
1. Print the housing in **PETG**, any colour (not PLA): the Pi bay may reach PLA's softening
   temperature.

Notes, not conditions:
- **SGP40:** only the hottest, wettest corner of the range (35 °C with 80 %RH) is just past the
  sensor's humidity-compensation limit. The sensor still runs; the VOC index may read less
  accurately there. Monitoring only (REQ-015), so accepted.
- **Supply:** no operating temperature is published. It sits in room air, not in the housing,
  so the room range applies; a UL-listed wall adapter of this kind is normally rated to at
  least 40 °C. Accepted as low risk.

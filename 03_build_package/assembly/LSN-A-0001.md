# Assembly instruction: LSN-A-0001 Lab sensor node

## Overview
A Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W and two Qwiic sensor boards (BME280 temperature, humidity and pressure;
SGP40 VOC) in a two-part printed housing (base and snap-on lid), with the Pi and sensors in
separate bays. No soldering.

Pictures: renders from the Fusion assembly `LSN-A-0001 Lab sensor node` are added for the G2
review; photos replace them after the first build.

## Before you start
- **Parts:** as listed for LSN-A-0001 in `bom.csv`. Housing printed in PETG (AN-001).
- **Tools:** heat-set insert tool or soldering iron with an M2 insert tip; small Phillips screwdriver;
  a computer with Raspberry Pi Imager and an SD card reader.
- **Consumables:** serial number label.
- **Safety:** the insert tool is hot (about 230 °C for PETG); let the base cool before handling.
  Handle boards by their edges.

## Steps

### 1. Prepare the SD card
- **Parts:** ECX-0001 (1)
- **Do:** Flash Raspberry Pi OS Lite (64-bit) with Raspberry Pi Imager, hostname = the unit's
  serial number (SOP-002 section 7). Follow steps 1 to 7 of `software/lsn-node/README.md` once the
  unit is assembled; step 8 (overlay) last.
- **Check:** no Wi-Fi password or server address is written into any file in the repo (REQ-010).

### 2. Install the heat-set inserts
- **Parts:** LSN-0002 (1), HWN-0006 (12)
- **Do:** Press one M2 insert into each of the 12 boss holes in the base: 4 in the Pi bay and 8
  in the sensor bay.
- **Setting:** insert tool at the filament's insert temperature (about 230 °C for PETG). Press
  straight down until the insert is flush with the boss top.
- **Check:** every insert is flush and square; none is pushed through the floor.

### 3. Fit the Qwiic SHIM to the Pi
- **Parts:** ECM-0004 (1), ECM-0003 (1)
- **Do:** Push the SHIM down onto GPIO pins 1 to 6 (the header end nearest the SD slot) until it
  rests on the header's black plastic, with its board lying over the Pi.
- **Check:** pin 1 (square pad) is under the SHIM's pin-1 hole, and the SHIM's connector points
  toward the end wall.

### 4. Mount the Pi
- **Parts:** the Pi with SHIM, M2 x 6 nylon pan-head screws (4)
- **Do:** Place the Pi on the 4 bosses in the Pi bay, connectors toward the front wall. Fit the
  4 screws.
- **Check:** the micro-USB, mini-HDMI and SD slot line up with their openings; the screws are
  snug, not overtightened (nylon).

### 5. Mount the sensors
- **Parts:** ECM-0001 (1), ECM-0002 (1), M2 x 6 nylon pan-head screws (7)
- **Do:** Fit the BME280 on the 4 bosses at the end-wall side of the sensor bay with 3 screws (it
  has only 3 holes; one boss stays empty). Fit the SGP40 on the other 4 bosses with 4 screws.
- **Check:** each board's two Qwiic connectors face along the bay (toward the end walls).

### 6. Connect the cables
- **Parts:** ECW-0001 (2)
- **Do:** Plug one cable into the SHIM, route it along the end wall and through the notch at the
  end of the partition, and plug it into the BME280's nearer connector. Plug the second cable
  between the BME280's other connector and the SGP40.
- **Check:** connectors fully latched; the cable lies in the notch, below the rim, and does not
  touch the Pi's processor.

### 7. Insert the SD card and fit the lid
- **Parts:** ECX-0001 (1), LSN-0003 (1)
- **Do:** Insert the SD card through the end-wall opening. Press the lid on until all four snaps
  click.
- **Check:** the lid sits flat on the rim; the lid vents over the sensor bay are on the sensor
  side.

### 8. Label and power up
- **Parts:** ECP-0001 (1)
- **Do:** Apply the serial number label to the base. Plug in the wall supply.
- **Check:** within 2 min the test receiver shows readings from this unit.

## Final checks
- [ ] All 11 screws fitted and snug
- [ ] Lid snapped on all four sides
- [ ] Unit fits within 100 x 100 x 100 mm (REQ-007)
- [ ] No soldered joints added (REQ-008)
- [ ] Serial number label applied (SOP-002 section 7)

# Assembly instruction: LSN-A-0001 Lab sensor node

The instruction is the picture guide [LSN-A-0001_guide.html](LSN-A-0001_guide.html): parts
gallery, before-you-start, 7 steps and final checks. It is generated from
[steps.json](steps.json) and `../bom.csv` by `python build_guide.py` (add `--embed` for a
single file to share; print it to PDF from a browser for paper). The script stops if the
steps do not use every BOM line exactly once. Pictures are renders of the Fusion assembly in
[images/](images/); photos replace them after the first build where clearer.

Callouts are in the `refdes` column of `bom.csv`: P1 base, P2 lid, B1 Pi, B2 SD card, B3 SHIM,
B4 BME280, B5 SGP40, B6 wall supply, H1 `M2` insert, F1 `M2×5 pan`, W1 Qwiic cable.

## Buildability check in CAD (2026-11-25, agent, Fusion LSN-A-0001 v28)

Checks from the assembly-instructions skill, rev B:

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Order | Pass: every part is joined only to parts fitted in earlier steps |
| Insertion path | Pass: boards drop straight in. Pi ports clear the front wall by 0.4 mm only |
| Tool access | Pass: Ø4 screwdriver shaft clear at all 8 screws; Ø6 insert tip and Ø12 iron clear at all 8 inserts |
| Connectors and openings | Micro-USB window 25.6 × 9 mm and mini-HDMI window 16 × 10 mm take moulded plugs. SD card: the slot is 14 mm inside the end wall (opening 14 × 5 mm), so the card goes in at step 2, before the Pi is mounted; later swaps need tweezers or the lid off |
| Cables | B3 to B4 through the notch about 62 mm plus plugs: fits 100 mm. B4 to B5 about 26 mm: 100 mm leaves about 70 mm spare in the sensor bay. SHIM and BME280 connector positions are from unconfirmed models |
| Closing | Pass: tallest part (Pi header, 16 mm) is below the lid underside (20 mm); spare cable has about 9 mm |
| Instruction against BOM | Pass (`build_guide.py`) |
| Pictures | Every step has a render |

Not covered: hands, cable stiffness, plug latching force. The first build is the final check.

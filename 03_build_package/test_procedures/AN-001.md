# AN-001 Operating environment (15 to 35 °C, 20 to 80 %RH non-condensing)

Every component must be rated for the room range plus the temperature rise inside the housing.
Estimated rise: 10 to 20 °C in the Pi bay (about 1 W in a closed box; agent estimate, not
measured; TP-004 logs the CPU temperature). The sensor bay is separated by the partition and
vented, so it runs near room temperature.

| Part | Rated operating range | Worst case here | OK? | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ECM-0004 Pi Zero 2 W | 0 to 50 °C ambient (to confirm) | up to 55 °C in the Pi bay | **Check** | Raspberry Pi product brief: confirm the figure |
| ECM-0001 BME280 | -40 to 85 °C, 0 to 100 %RH | 35 °C, 80 %RH | Yes | Bosch BME280 datasheet |
| ECM-0002 SGP40 | -10 to 50 °C, 0 to 90 %RH (to confirm) | 35 °C, 80 %RH | Yes | Sensirion SGP40 datasheet: confirm |
| ECM-0003 SHIM, ECW-0001 cable | passive | - | Yes | - |
| ECP-0001 power supply | about 0 to 40 °C ambient (to confirm) | 35 °C, outside the housing | Yes | PiShop listing: confirm |
| ECX-0001 SD card | -25 to 85 °C | up to 55 °C | Yes | SanDisk Extreme spec |
| LSN-0001/0002 printed housing | PLA softens near 55 °C; PETG about 70 °C | up to 55 °C inside | **PLA marginal** | Material data |

## Result
Meets REQ-006 with two conditions, both carried to the G2 review:
1. Print the housing in **PETG** (not PLA): the Pi bay may reach PLA's softening temperature.
2. Confirm the Pi's ambient rating. If it is 0 to 50 °C, the Pi-bay estimate (up to 55 °C at a
   35 °C room) is over it: TP-004 must show the CPU temperature stays below the Pi's throttling
   point (80 °C).

Ratings marked "to confirm" are from memory and must be checked against the datasheets before
release.
